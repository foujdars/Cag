01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

The insight you need may already exist in another state.

Years of audit observations hold practical lessons. Yet finding a relevant precedent often depends on knowing the right report, state, or keyword in advance.

Audit Atlas is designed to make that existing public knowledge easier to discover. Search an issue in plain language, see related observations across states, and follow the trail to the published CAG source.
